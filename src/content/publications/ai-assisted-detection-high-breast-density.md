abstract: "Retrospective comparative study of AI-assisted malignant breast cancer detection in a cohort enriched for high breast density, using biopsy-confirmed outcomes, inter-reader BI-RADS agreement, and commercial AI risk classification at Hanoi Medical University Hospital."
plainLanguageSummary: "This follow-up to the ECR 2024 study focuses on the subgroup where mammography is weakest: dense breasts with a suspicious finding. A retrospective 12-month Hanoi Medical University Hospital cohort of 443 patients (BI-RADS 4, 5 and selected 3) was assessed against biopsy-confirmed outcomes — 136 negative cases, 292 with unilateral lesions of which 138 were malignant, and 15 bilateral cases with 3 malignant. Inter-rater agreement between readers was ICC 0.718–0.780 (p < 0.001). The commercial AI independently identified 97% of positive patients correctly, while classifying 21% of biopsied patients as low risk."
contribution: "Quantifies both sides of AI support in a dense-breast cohort with biopsy-confirmed ground truth: the cases it catches, and the biopsied patients it would have called low risk."
method: "Retrospective comparative analysis of biopsy-confirmed outcomes, inter-reader BI-RADS agreement measured by intraclass correlation, and commercial AI risk classification on a cohort enriched for high breast density."

relevance: "The 97% figure and the 21% figure have to be read together. Reporting only the first would be the kind of selective quotation that makes clinical AI claims untrustworthy."
limitation: "Retrospective, single-centre, enriched cohort published as a conference e-poster. An enriched cohort inflates prevalence relative to a screening population, so these numbers do not transfer directly to screening."
