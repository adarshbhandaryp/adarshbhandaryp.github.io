abstract: "Retrospective external evaluation of a breast MRI AI research prototype on multi-sequence 3T examinations from West China Hospital, with expert lesion annotations, reporting case-level sensitivity and per-case false-positive burden."
plainLanguageSummary: "A breast MRI AI prototype developed in Europe was evaluated retrospectively on an external Chinese cohort — the kind of test that decides whether a model generalizes or merely fits its development population. 200 cases were sampled from a 4,000-patient West China Hospital breast MRI cohort acquired between November 2017 and August 2023, using multi-sequence 3T MRI with expert lesion annotations. Case-level sensitivity was 0.906, with a median of 0 false positives per case, an average of 0.635, and a maximum of 7 in a single case. This work came out of a guest research stay at the hospital."
contribution: "External-cohort evidence for a breast MRI detection prototype, with the false-positive distribution reported rather than only the sensitivity."

relevance: "For breast MRI to be usable at volume, sensitivity alone is not the constraint — the false-positive burden per case is what determines whether radiologists can work with the output."
