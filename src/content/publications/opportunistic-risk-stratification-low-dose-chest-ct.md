plainLanguageSummary: "Low-dose chest CT is already acquired at scale for lung screening, and it happens to include breast tissue. This proof-of-concept study applies multiple instance learning to that existing data for opportunistic breast cancer risk stratification, stratified against BI-RADS 3–6 categories on a cohort of 60 patients. The individual-breast model reached 72.7% accuracy with AUC 0.85, and the bilateral model 75.0% accuracy with AUC 0.87. The authors describe it as the first comprehensive proof-of-concept of its kind. I co-supervised the master's thesis this paper grew out of and co-authored the result."
